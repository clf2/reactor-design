DESIGN DECISION — WHY THIS ARRANGEMENT
Recommended architecture: Use the first concept for the normal 15-day operating period: fill the reactor once, isolate the feed tank, and continuously recirculate inside the reactor loop. This avoids repeatedly sending the culture back through the feed tank.

Flow control: Use manual globe valves (FCV-01 to FCV-12) on each branch for throttling and balancing. Manual globe valves are used here for throttling and loop-to-loop balancing; final valve Cv/ΔP must be verified at the selected operating flow.

Important hydraulic note: P-102 is in a closed liquid circuit. The top-vs-bottom elevation is not a net static head requirement around a closed loop; the pump mainly supplies friction/minor losses. The current 27.8103 L/h value is the user's HRT flow basis, not a finalized internal circulation rate.

Engineering status: Preliminary process/design representation. Final P-102 selection requires an agreed internal recirculation flow, verified fitting losses, valve Cv, operating pressure and NPSH/vendor data.
