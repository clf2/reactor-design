1FILL / MAKE-UP
TK-101 → P-101 → liquid headers. Fill the reactor, then isolate the feed source.
2HEADER DISTRIBUTION
23.6387 L/h HRT turnover basis divides into 2 sections: 11.8194 L/h each.
3LOOP DISTRIBUTION
12 equal loops receive approximately 1.9699 L/h each on the nominal HRT basis.
4BOTTOM COLLECTION
Loop outlets collect at the bottom and return to the closed recirculation circuit.
5BOTTOM GAS INJECTION
12.7501 Nm³/h flue gas at 10% CO₂ enters through the bottom gas line / sparger basis.
6P-102 RECIRCULATION
P-102 continuously recirculates culture internally; final pump duty is not frozen.
715-DAY BATCH
The reactor operates closed for 15 days with internal recirculation and batch harvest at the end.
