Specified Reactor Envelope
5.8 m × 3.4 m × 1.8 m
Engineering check: Adjacent tube centerline pitch = 2 × U-bend CLR = 2 × 0.165 = 0.330 m.
For 49 tubes: 48 × 0.330 = 15.84 m minimum serpentine span.
Conclusion: A true 49-tube / 48-U-bend loop cannot be force-fitted into a 3.4 m × 1.8 m envelope while retaining 1500 mm tube length and 165 mm CLR. A final fabrication design must revise routing, tube count, CLR, or envelope.
